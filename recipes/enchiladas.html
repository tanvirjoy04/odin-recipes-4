Enchiladas

Home

Overview

Tender shredded chicken wrapped in soft corn tortillas, smothered with a rich, flavorful enchilada sauce, and topped with melted cheese. These enchiladas are baked to perfection for a comforting, satisfying> meal that's full of Mexican-inspired flavor. Perfect for weeknights or sharing with family and friends.

Ingredients

  • 1 tablespoon olive oil
  • 4 skinless, boneless chicken breast halves
  • 1 onion, chopped
  • 1 3/4 cups shredded Cheddar cheese
  • 1/2 pint sour cream
  • 1 tablespoon dried parsley
  • 1/2 teaspoon dried oregano
  • 1/2 teaspoon ground black pepper
  • 1 can tomato sauce
  • 1/3 cup chopped green bell pepper
  • 1 clove garlic, minced
  • 1 tablespoon chili powder
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt
  • 1/2 cup water
  • 8 flour tortillas
  • 1 jar taco sauce

Steps

  1. Gather all ingredients and preheat the oven to 350 degrees F
  2. Heat oil in a nonstick skillet over medium heat. Add chicken and cook until no longer pink and juices run clear, 5 to 7 minutes per side. Transfer chicken to a cutting board and slice into cubes. Return chicken to the skillet.
  3. Add onion, 1 cup Cheddar cheese, sour cream, parsley, oregano, and black pepper. Cook and stir over low heat until cheese melts. Stir in tomato sauce, green pepper, garlic, chili powder, and salt. Add water if needed.
  4. Spoon chicken mixture into tortillas and roll into enchiladas. Arrange enchiladas, seam-side down, in a 9 x 13 inch baking dish.
  5. Top with taco sauce and remaining 3/4 cup Cheddar cheese.
  6. Bake in the preheated oven, uncovered, until cheese has melted, about 20 minutes. Let cool briefly before serving.
  7. Serve and enjoy!
